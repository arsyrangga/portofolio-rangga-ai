Welcome to Novelai ("App", "Service", "we", "our", or "us"), published on Google Play by RANGGA AI. Novelai is an artificial intelligence-assisted mobile application designed to help authors, novelists, and storytellers brainstorm, write, generate, read, and share episodic novels and interactive stories.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, disclose, and protect your information when you access or use our Android mobile application. By installing or using Novelai, you agree to the collection and handling of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide, personalize, and continually improve the Novelai experience.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Credentials: When you register an account, we collect your display name, email address, and an encrypted hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Guest Account Identifier: If you use Novelai without registering (Guest Mode), we generate an anonymous cryptographic device fingerprint to maintain your guest novels, reading progress, and coin balance across app restarts.
  • User-Generated Creative Content: Story titles, genres, character names, character physical appearances, descriptions, story synopses, custom chapter prompt instructions, and authored chapter content that you input or generate.
  • Social Interactions: Public comments, likes, and reading records on community novels that you choose to interact with.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and App Performance Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, network connection status, and diagnostic crash logs to help us identify and resolve bugs.
  • Advertising Identifier: Google Advertising ID (AAID) accessed via the Google Mobile Ads SDK for serving rewarded advertisements.
Device Permissions: Novelai only requires INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions to synchronize stories and connect to AI engines. Novelai does NOT request, access, or record your Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, or Device Storage.

2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

Novelai leverages state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) via the DeepSeek API to stream and compose novel chapters based on your direction.

  • When you request chapter generation, the title, genre, character details, chapter instructions, and an excerpt of the preceding chapter are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS/TLS to the AI processing engine.
  • Your story prompts are used solely to generate the requested chapter narrative for you.
  • We do not sell your personal creative stories or prompts to data brokers.

3. Monetization, In-App Purchases, & Advertisements

A. Google Play In-App Billing

Users may purchase coin packages to generate chapters. All financial transactions and payment processing are handled directly and securely by Google Play In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing). We never collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial credentials. We only receive cryptographically signed purchase tokens from Google to credit coins to your in-app wallet.

B. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Novelai offers optional Rewarded Advertisements that allow users to earn free coins by watching short video ads. We integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect and process information such as IP addresses, Google Advertising ID (AAID), and ad interaction telemetry in compliance with Google's policies.

You can manage or reset your personalized advertising preferences at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Third-Party Services

Novelai relies on trusted third-party service providers to power specific app functionalities. Each third-party provider operates under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • DeepSeek API: https://www.deepseek.com

5. Data Retention & User Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the Service.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any novel or chapter you have created directly within the application. Once deleted, the content is permanently removed from our production database.
  • Account & Personal Data Deletion Request: If you wish to permanently delete your registered account, profile data, coin wallet, and all associated novels, you can request full account deletion at any time by emailing us at ranggantwleng77@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Your account and all associated data will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

6. Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information. All network communication between the Novelai Android app, our production backend servers, and third-party AI APIs is strictly encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS). Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed.

However, please bear in mind that no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% infallible, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Novelai is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or the minimum legal age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, we will promptly delete such information from our records. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
